Privacy Policy

Bespoke Discretion & Security Protocol

1. Privacy Commitment

At AURA Architecture & Luxury Estates, we hold client confidentiality and data security in the highest regard. Our estates are built on trusts of absolute privacy, and our digital records follow the same strict guidelines.

2. Information Collection

We collect details exclusively relevant to customizing architectural blueprints, planning specifications, and styling consults. This includes:

  • Identity information: Full name, secure email address, and direct phone contact.
  • Project profiles: Design scopes, location preferences, and blueprint metadata.

3. Data Discretion

Your records and planning blueprints are shared only with the assigned Principal Architect and select structural partners. We never license, sell, or publicize any client data or design concepts without explicit, signed consent.

4. Secure Storage

All digital blueprints and client profile files are stored on isolated, encrypted servers with biometric access limitations to ensure the safety of your estate layouts.
