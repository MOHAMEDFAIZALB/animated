Architectural Engagement Guidelines

1. Scope of Engagement

Engagement with AURA begins upon submission of a request for consultation. A Principal Architect evaluates project parameters and schedules an exclusive design audit prior to drafting structural layouts.

2. Intellectual Property

All structural designs, custom render states, and digital interactive blueprints generated during client interactions are the property of AURA Architecture until a construction contract has been signed and finalized.

3. Estimates and Budgets

Architectural budgets and structural quotes are dynamic, depending on materials selection (e.g. Italian marble varieties, custom glazing finishes) and local municipal guidelines. All blueprint deadlines are targets and may vary with environmental conditions.

4. Governing Laws

Any architectural contracts or blueprint requests executed under the AURA brand are governed by the local construction laws and luxury planning guidelines of our design headquarters.
